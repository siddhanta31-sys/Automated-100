# Trend2Sketch Product Intelligence Upgrade

Adds four high-priority capabilities on top of Design Director R&D:

1. **My Design DNA** — persistent owner-approved reference-image library with notes. Deep research receives the reference directions and is explicitly told to learn characteristics, never copy a specific piece.
2. **Stone & Weight Engineering** — persistent target gold-weight range, stone strategy, and target market/customer controls.
3. **Novelty Gate** — adjustable 35–95 repetition gate applied before expensive rendering, comparing concepts with previous Trend2Sketch output.
4. **CAD Handoff Sheet** — each newly rendered design stores and displays dimensions, stone hierarchy/sizes, setting, construction, articulation, comfort, lightweighting and manufacturability notes.

Recommended first validation: Deep mode, 10 designs, quality threshold 75, novelty gate 72. Add at least 10 gold-standard references with concise notes before judging the learning loop.
